# Security and privacy

## How your data is protected

- **Encryption at rest.** Files are stored with AES-256 encryption. Your content
  is not readable from the storage layer.
- **Encryption in transit.** Every connection uses TLS 1.2 or later.
- **Zero knowledge of your content.** We store the encrypted file and the keys
  needed to sync it, in separate systems, so a compromise of one does not expose
  your files.
- **Access control.** Support staff cannot open your files. If a request would
  require that, we ask for your explicit written consent first, and we log it.

## Certifications and audits

- **SOC 2 Type II.** Our controls are audited annually. The report is available
  to customers on request under NDA.
- **Penetration testing.** An independent test runs every year. Findings are
  tracked to closure.

## Account security

- Turn on two-factor authentication under **Account > Security**. We strongly
  recommend it on every account, and it is required on Business.
- Password reset links expire after 1 hour.
- Signing out of all devices ends every active session immediately.
- We will never ask for your password, and we will never call asking for it. A
  caller who does is not CloudSync Pro.

## What we collect

Account details, billing records, and the technical data needed to run the
service: IP address, device and app version, and sync events. We do not sell
personal data.

## Reporting a problem

If you think your account has been compromised, or you believe there has been a
security breach, open a ticket with **security** in the subject and we will
treat it as urgent. Change your password and sign out of all devices while you
wait. This is handled by a human, not the automated assistant.

## Common questions

**Can an employee read my files?**
No. Support staff cannot open your files without your explicit written consent,
and that consent is logged.

**Do you have a SOC 2 report?**
Yes, and we share it with customers under NDA. Ask support.

**What happens to my files if I cancel?**
They stay on your devices, and you can keep downloading them. Version history and
sharing links stop when your paid period ends.
