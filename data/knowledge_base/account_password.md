# Account and password

## Signing in

Sign in from the desktop app, the mobile app, or the web dashboard at
cloudsync.example.com with the email address on your account.

If you are signed in on more devices than your plan allows, the extra devices
can sign in but will not sync until you sign out somewhere.

## Resetting your password

1. Open the sign-in screen and choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address on the account.
3. Open the email and choose **Reset password**.
4. Choose a new password.

**The reset link expires after 1 hour.** After that you have to request a new
one. This is deliberate: a link that stays valid forever is a link someone else
can use.

If the link has expired, request another one from the same **Forgot password**
screen. There is no limit on how many reset emails you can ask for.

## Changing your email address

Open **Account > Profile > Email** and confirm with your current password. The
new address receives receipts and password resets from then on.

## Two-factor authentication

Turn on 2FA under **Account > Security > Two-factor authentication**. You can
use any authenticator app. Keep your recovery codes somewhere safe: they are the
only way back in if you lose your device.

## Signing out everywhere

**Account > Security > Sign out of all devices** ends every active session,
including the one you are using. You will need to sign in again.

## Common questions

**The reset link says it has expired.**
Links are valid for 1 hour. Request a new one from **Forgot password**; the new
link works immediately.

**I am locked out after too many attempts.**
The lock clears on its own after 30 minutes, or immediately once you use a
password reset link.

**I forgot which email I signed up with.**
Look for the welcome email. If you cannot find it, the account may be under a
work address that has since changed, and support can confirm the address on file
once you pass an identity check.

**How do I change my password if I am signed in?**
**Account > Security > Change password.** You need your current password.
