# CloudSync Pro

CloudSync Pro keeps your files in sync across every device you use, and keeps a
history of every version for 30 days.

## What it does

- Automatic sync across 5 devices, so a file you save on your laptop is on your
  phone before you pick it up.
- Offline mode: you can read and edit files with no connection, and they sync
  the next time you are online.
- Version history: keep 30 days of file versions, and restore any of them.
- Sharing: share a folder with a link, or with named people, and revoke access
  at any time.
- Cross-platform clients for desktop and mobile.

## How syncing works

The desktop app watches your sync folder. When a file changes, it uploads the
change and tells your other devices to download it. Most changes are visible on
every device within a few seconds.

The sync folder shows a small status marker per file:

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Tick | Synced to every device |
| Circle | Uploading or downloading now |
| Exclamation mark | This file could not be synced. See troubleshooting. |

## What is in the box

Every plan includes the desktop and mobile apps, offline mode, 30 days of
version history, AES-256 encryption at rest, and email support. Plans differ in
storage, number of devices, and maximum file size.

## Common questions

**Does CloudSync Pro work without an internet connection?**
Yes. Offline mode keeps reading and editing available, and changes are synced
when you reconnect.

**Can I access my files from another computer?**
Yes. Sign in on any supported platform and your sync folder appears. The number
of devices you can sync from at once depends on your plan.

**Do you offer a public API?**
Not in v1. Contact support if you need to script something against your account
and we will tell you what is possible today.

**Can I share a folder outside my team?**
Yes. Share it with a link, or with named people, and revoke access at any time
from the sharing panel.
