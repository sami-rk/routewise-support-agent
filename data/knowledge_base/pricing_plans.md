# Pricing and plans

CloudSync Pro has three plans. They differ in storage, how many devices can sync
at the same time, and the largest file you can upload.

## The plans

| Plan | Price | Storage | Devices | Max file size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $9/mo | 100 GB | 2 | 2 GB |
| Pro | $19/mo | 1 TB | 5 | 10 GB |
| Business | $49/mo | 5 TB | Unlimited | 10 GB |

The Pro plan is $19 per month, includes 1 TB of storage, and syncs across 5
devices at the same time.

Business is the only plan with unlimited devices. Device count is the number of
devices that can sync simultaneously, not the number of devices you have ever
signed in from.

## Billing

- Plans are billed monthly in advance, in USD.
- Your subscription renews automatically on the same day each month.
- You can change plan at any time. An upgrade takes effect immediately and is
  charged as a prorated amount. A downgrade takes effect at the end of the
  current billing period.
- Prices shown are the list prices. There is no annual discount and no
  promotional pricing on these plans.

## Changing plan

Open **Account > Subscription > Change plan** and pick the new plan. The
confirmation screen shows the amount and the date the change takes effect.

## Common questions

**What happens if I go over my storage?**
Uploads pause until you free space or upgrade. Your existing files are never
deleted, and downloads keep working.

**Is there a discount for yearly billing?**
No. These plans are monthly only.

**How many devices can sync at the same time on Pro?**
5. Additional devices can sign in, but only 5 sync at once.

**What is the difference between Basic and Pro?**
Basic is $9/mo with 100 GB and 2 devices. Pro is $19/mo with 1 TB, 5 devices,
and a 10 GB file limit instead of 2 GB.

**Can I try Pro before paying?**
There is no free trial on these plans. Cancel any time and you keep access until
the end of the period you paid for.
