# Cancellation and refunds

## Cancelling your subscription

You can cancel at any time, from **Account > Subscription > Cancel subscription**.
There is no cancellation fee and no phone call.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep access to your files until the end
of the period you have already paid for, then your sync folder stops syncing.
Your files stay on your devices, and you can still download them, but the
version history and sharing links stop working once the subscription ends.

We will ask you to confirm the cancellation before it runs, so a mistaken
message cannot cancel your plan.

## Refunds

A charge is refundable within 14 days of the charge date. Inside that window you
get a full refund of the amount charged.

- The 14 days are counted from the day you were charged, not from the day you
  noticed the problem.
- A refund returns the charge to your original payment method. Your bank usually
  shows it within 5 to 10 business days.
- A refund also cancels the subscription that charge was for, because you are no
  longer paying for it.
- Refunds above $20 need a support specialist to approve, which usually takes a
  few hours. Refunds of $20 or less are issued immediately.
- A charge older than 14 days is not refundable, including for a service outage.
  Tell us what happened anyway and we will look at it as a service issue.

## What is not refundable

- Charges older than 14 days.
- Charges that have already been refunded.
- Add-ons and one-off purchases; those follow their own terms.

## Common questions

**Can I get a refund under your 14-day policy?**
Yes, if the charge is 14 days old or less. Tell us the invoice and the reason,
and we will process it.

**Will I lose my files if I cancel?**
No. Your files stay on your devices. You lose version history and sharing links
once the paid period ends.

**Do you refund a partial period?**
No. Plans are billed monthly in advance, so we refund whole charges, not the
unused part of a month.

**How long does a refund take?**
The refund is issued straight away for $20 or less, and after specialist
approval for more. Your bank usually shows the money within 5 to 10 business
days.
