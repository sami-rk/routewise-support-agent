# Limits and quotas

## Storage

| Plan | Storage |
|---|---|
| Basic | 100 GB |
| Pro | 1 TB |
| Business | 5 TB |

Uploads pause when you reach your storage limit. Your files are never deleted
because you went over, and downloads keep working. Free space, or upgrade, to
resume uploading.

## Devices

Devices are counted as devices syncing at the same time.

| Plan | Devices syncing at once |
|---|---|
| Basic | 2 |
| Pro | 5 |
| Business | Unlimited |

A device over the allowance can sign in and download, but does not sync, until
you sign out on another device. Sign out from **Account > Security > Devices**.

## Maximum file size

| Plan | Max file size |
|---|---|
| Basic | 2 GB |
| Pro | 10 GB |
| Business | 10 GB |

A file over the limit fails and does not retry. Split it, or move up a plan.

## Version history

30 days of file versions are kept on every plan, including deleted files.
Restoring a version brings the file back as a new change, so nothing is lost
when you restore.

## Sharing links

A sharing link stays active for 30 days. You can revoke a link at any time from
the sharing panel, and revoking it takes effect immediately.

## Quotas that do not apply

There is no cap on the number of files, the number of sharing links you can
create, or how often you can restore a version. The only limits are storage,
devices and file size.

## Common questions

**What happens when I hit my storage limit?**
Uploads pause. Nothing is deleted, and downloads keep working.

**Do deleted files count towards storage?**
Until version history expires at 30 days, yes. Empty the trash from version
history to free the space sooner.

**Why did my sync stop on a new device?**
You are at the device limit for your plan. Basic allows 2 and Pro allows 5.
